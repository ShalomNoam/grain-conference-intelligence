# Video walkthrough — talking outline (5–10 min)

Not a script to read verbatim — a structure so nothing gets missed and the
video stays under 10 minutes. Record with the deployed live URL, not
localhost.

## 1. Cold open (30s)
"This is a Conference Intelligence tool for Grain's sales team — decide which
conferences to prioritize, plan coverage across the year, capture leads fast
in the field, and recognize when the same contact across multiple conferences
is warming up or just being polite."

## 2. Conferences + scoring (2 min)
- Open `/conferences`. Filter to `fx-treasury`, point out Money20/20 USA
  scoring S-tier vs. Web Summit scoring C-tier despite 5x the headcount.
- Click "How is the score calculated?" — walk through the 5 weights out loud
  in your own words: vertical fit matters most because a huge audience with
  no PSPs/treasury buyers in it isn't worth attending; format and cost are
  about efficiency, not just fit.

## 3. Planning view (1.5 min)
- `/planning` — show a flagged quarter gap, then a clustering card (two
  conferences same region within a few weeks) and say what you'd actually do
  with that (combine into one trip).

## 4. Field capture (1 min)
- `/capture` on a phone-width window (or an actual phone). Log a lead in
  under 15 seconds to prove the friction point was taken seriously.

## 5. Cross-conference contacts (2–3 min) — the core of the assignment
- `/contacts` — show Dana Shapiro: 2+ touches, promoted between conferences,
  temperature rising → "warming" nudge. Explain the rule-based signal is
  instant and free; click "AI relationship summary" and read the narrative it
  adds.
- Walk through the edge cases out loud: name typos/nicknames, job changes
  (show the company-history trail), and the review queue for a fuzzy match
  you're not fully confident about — emphasize *why* it's a human prompt
  instead of a silent auto-merge or a silently dropped duplicate.
- Push one interaction to HubSpot, show the synced badge.

## 6. AI tool use, honestly (1 min)
- Which tool(s) you used to build this, what they were fast at (scaffolding,
  boilerplate, the matching/scoring logic once you specified the rules), and
  one concrete place they got in the way or produced something you had to
  correct — specific beats vague here.

## 7. What's next (1 min)
- Pick 2–3 real items from the README's "Trade-offs & what I'd build next"
  section — say *why* each one, not just what.

## 8. FX calculator (30s, only if time remains)
- `/calculator` — mention explicitly it's a bonus, not one of the 7 core
  requirements, and why you added it (something concrete to show a prospect
  mid-conversation).
